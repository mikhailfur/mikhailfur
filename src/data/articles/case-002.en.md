A local laboratory begins not with the most powerful hardware, but with a clear distribution of tasks. A text model, image generation, and the interface do not have to run simultaneously at maximum settings.

I prefer to first identify the bottleneck: **video card memory**, disk speed, or waiting time. After that, it is easier to choose the model quantization, image size, and task queue without magic or random compromises.

The most valuable thing about local deployment is **control over the process**. You can experiment with the chain, save successful settings, and return to them without depending on an external service.
