Three things I added to the site.

**Chat.** An anonymous chat roulette. Pick a nickname and wait for a random stranger. No profiles, no sign-up. Messages live for an hour; a reported transcript is kept for seven days.

**Snake.** The classic snake on a 20×20 board. Swipe on a phone, arrow keys on a keyboard. Score and best run stay in the browser.

**Wallz.** A race to the far edge of the board. You and a bot take turns, and you can place walls. It opens in the same arcade window as snake and chat.

All three start from the terminal with `snake`, `wallz` and `chat`, or from the Play menu. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/mikhailfur).
