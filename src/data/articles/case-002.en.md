**Special Containment Procedures**: The access point is sealed and disguised as terminal commands. Access to the interactive functions of I-15 is permitted to all personnel with clearance level 1 and above exclusively via a **network web terminal**.

2. "SNAKE" Simulation Module: An interactive simulator of recursive data absorption ("Snake") invoked by the command **play.** Use is recommended for testing reaction time and assessing the **mental stability** of employees during breaks between shift duties.

3. "WALLZ" Spatial Protocol: An interactive tactical environment functioning according to the algorithms of the Wallz autonomous platform, also invoked by the command **play.** It performs simulations of territory control and **zone capture**.
