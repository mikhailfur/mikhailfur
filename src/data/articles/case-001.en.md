I am not interested in the model itself, but in the moment when it stops being a separate browser window and becomes part of a process. A good tool reduces the distance between an idea and the first working result.

That is why I build small chains: a **local model** helps break down a task, an image generator verifies the visual direction, and automation removes repetitive actions. Each part must be clear and replaceable.

The main criterion is simple: after setup, **the system should help me think**, rather than requiring constant maintenance. If I have to remember too many commands, interfaces, and exceptions, the tool is not yet complete.
